## The priority of the week

**`main` becomes the source of truth of the published package again.** Today it is
not: npm serves `0.1.65`, the platform installs it, and no branch in this repo
contains that version. Until this closes, every publish is made blind — there is
nothing to diff the tarball against, nothing to revert to, and no way to review
what production is running.

**How we know it was met:** on Friday, `npm view arxatec-ui version` and the
`version` in `main`'s `package.json` are the same number, and the content of the
published tarball matches `main`'s `src/`. If the two numbers differ, it was not met
— regardless of how much was learned along the way.
